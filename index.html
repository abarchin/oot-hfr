OoT HFR Patcher (fork)

Runs Ocarina of Time at 20, up to 30 or up to 60 fps on N64 hardware, with game speed unchanged. You choose the rate on the file select screen or in the pause menu.

This is a fork of 64-bites/oot-hfr, not the official patcher. It applies the same HFR v0.5.0 patch, then adds the options below. Turn both off to get the original HFR ROM. Source and details: abarchin/oot-hfr.

Extra changes in this fork

Each option is a small patch applied on top of HFR. Both are on by default.

Your ROM stays on this computer. Nothing is uploaded.
You need
  • Your own ROM of the USA 1.0 version. No ROM comes with this patch.
  • A flash cart or an emulator to run the result.
  • An Expansion Pak for any rate above Standard. Without one, the game runs as Standard.

Rates depend on your console and the scene. The higher rates are for overclocked hardware. At stock speed, expect mostly 20 fps. This is a fan project, not affiliated with Nintendo. Patching uses Rom Patcher JS modules.
